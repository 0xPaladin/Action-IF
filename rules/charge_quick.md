# CHARGE SRD — QUICK REFERENCE

## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY & STRUCTURE
- **Fiction-first**: Rules are tools to resolve uncertainty; always look at the fiction before the mechanics.
- **Collaborative storytelling**: The GM controls the world and NPCs; Players control PCs. Everyone shapes the story.
- **Session structure**: Sessions are composed of **scenes** stitched together like a movie. Scenes are back-and-forth between GM description and player actions, resolved through dice rolls.
- **No NPC rolls**: NPCs succeed unless PCs try to stop them. The PC's action roll resolves both sides simultaneously.

---

## 2. CHARACTER CREATION

### Details (2–5 traits)
Descriptive traits that define narrative liberties. Defaults:
- **Concept**: Elevator-pitch of the character.
- **Appearance**: Looks, clothing, etc.
- **Ties**: 1–3 relations to people/organizations.

*Designer guidance*: Customize details to reinforce setting themes (e.g., noble house affiliations, magical power types).

### Actions (7 dots at creation, max 2 in any single action)
Default 12-action list:
| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Muscle** | Force to move, overcome, or wreck. |
| **Move** | Quickly shift position or escape danger. |
| **Finesse** | Dexterous manipulation or misdirection. |
| **Sneak** | Traverse skillfully and quietly. |
| **Shoot** | Carefully track and shoot a target. |
| **Tinker** | Understand, create, or repair mechanisms/organisms. |
| **Study** | Gather, scrutinize, and analyze information. |
| **Notice** | Observe and anticipate outcomes. |
| **Bond** | Reassure and socialize with friends/contacts. |
| **Command** | Compel swift obedience with skill/respect. |
| **Focus** | Concentrate for great mental strength. |
| **Sway** | Influence with guile, charm, or argument. |

*Designer guidance*: Change action names/number to fit aesthetic. Reduce starting dots if using fewer actions; recommended ratio: **(number of actions / 2) + 1**.

**Alternative action sets provided in SRD:**
- **4 dots across 6 actions**: Muscle, Finesse, Move, Study, Talk, Focus.
- **3 dots across 4 actions**: Strength, Agility, Intelligence, Charm.
- **2 dots across 3 actions**: Power, Speed, Focus.

### Talents
- Start with **zero talents**.
- Gained by reaching **milestones** (awarded by GM after big story events).
- A talent grants either:
  - +1d6 when **[condition]**
  - Lower risk when **[condition]**
  - Increase effect when **[condition]**
  - Use [action] instead of [action] when **[condition]**
  - New narrative liberty when **[condition]**

*Designer guidance*: A talent tree of 4–6 talents is sufficient for an average ~6 session campaign. Default Charge has 13 for generic flexibility, but smaller is usually better.

## 3. CORE MECHANICS

### The Dice System
- Uses **d6s only**.
- Roll a pool, read the **single highest die**.
- **6** = Full success.
- **4–5** = Partial success (consequence occurs).
- **1–3** = Bad outcome (goal fails + consequence).
- **66 (multiple 6s)** = Critical success (increased effect; +1d on next action if from determination roll).
- **Zero/negative dice**: Roll 2d6, take the **lowest**. Cannot roll criticals.

### Action Roll Procedure
Triggered when a PC faces an obstacle with risk of failure.

**Step 1 — Player Sets Goal**
- States goal and chosen action.
- Gathers d6s equal to that action's dot rating.

**Step 2 — GM Sets Effect Rating**
- **Great**: 3 ticks on success.
- **Standard**: 2 ticks on success.
- **Limited**: 1 tick on success.

**Step 3 — Player Evens the Odds (Bonus Dice)**
Max 2 bonus dice normally:
- **Assist**: Teammate spends **1 momentum**, describes help, grants **+1d6**. Assister exposes themselves to danger.
- **Push yourself**: Spend **2 momentum** for either **+1d6** or **improved effect**.
- **Add tension**: GM offers a consequence; if accepted, **+1d6**. (GM can decline if it doesn't fit.)

**Step 4 — Roll & Interpret**
- **66**: Critical success. Achieve goal with **double ticks**.
- **6**: Full success. Earn ticks based on effect.
- **4–5**: Partial success. Achieve goal, but suffer a consequence.
- **1–3**: Bad outcome. Goal fails, suffer a consequence.

**Momentum Generation from Action Rolls** (only if momentum was NOT spent on the roll):
- **4–5**: Gain **1** momentum.
- **6**: Gain **2** momentum.
- **66**: Gain **3** momentum.

### Momentum
- **Starts at 2 per session**.
- Spent to: boost actions, assist allies, resist consequences (determination rolls).
- **Spent momentum cannot generate momentum on that same roll.**

*Designer guidance* ("Momentum Dial"): Change when/how much momentum is gained to adjust tone. Higher = bigger-than-life; lower = grittier. This heavily impacts pushing yourself and resisting consequences.

## 4. BOOSTING ACTION ROLLS

### Push Yourself
Spend **2 momentum per push** for one of:
- +1d6 to pool.
- Increase effect by one level.
- Take action even if **taken out**.

### Add Tension
Ask GM *"What would adding tension look like?"*
- GM offers a consequence (danger clock ticks, stress, narrative drama).
- Accept = +1d6.
- GM can say no if it doesn't make sense.

---

## 5. TEAMWORK

| Maneuver | Cost | Effect |
|----------|------|--------|
| **Assist** | 1 momentum | +1d6 to ally's roll; assister exposed to danger. |
| **Protect** | — | Step in to suffer an ally's consequence. Can make determination roll to reduce/cancel it. |
| **Set Up** | — | Make action roll for indirect effect. On success, next teammate gets **improved effect OR reduced risk** (you choose). |

---

## 6. CONSEQUENCES

Suffered on **4–5** or **1–3**. GM chooses one:
1. **Reduced Effect**: Effect level drops by one.
2. **Complication**: Tick 2 segments on a new/existing **danger clock**.
3. **Escalated Challenge**: Increase current challenge clock by 2 segments.
4. **Lost Opportunity**: Attempt failed; need a different action.
5. **Worse Condition**: Take **2 stress**.

---

## 7. DETERMINATION ROLLS (Resisting Consequences)

When a player dislikes a consequence, they can resist.

**Procedure:**
- Build pool of **1d6 per momentum spent** (decide amount before rolling).
- Roll and interpret:
  - **1–3**: Consequence still happens fully.
  - **4–5**: Consequence is **reduced**.
  - **6**: Consequence is **avoided**.
  - **66**: Consequence avoided; gain **+1d on next action**.

*Designer guidance*: This is the "I don't think so" mechanic. The cost (momentum by default) forces meaningful sacrifice. Hacks could use stat rolls or sacrifice assets/details instead.

---

## 8. CLOCKS

Circular trackers in **4, 6, or 8 segments**.

### Progress Clocks
Track task progress or long-term projects.
- **Limited effect**: 1 tick.
- **Standard effect**: 2 ticks.
- **Great effect**: 3 ticks.
- **Critical success**: Double ticks.

### Danger Clocks
Track escalating threats/complications.
- Filled when a consequence ticks **2 segments** (from Complication or Escalated Challenge).
- When filled: narrative danger comes to fruition.

### Project Clocks
Used for long-term PC goals or repairing attachments.

---

## 9. HEALTH & CONDITION

### Stress
- Represented by a **4-segment stress clock ⨁** on the character sheet.
- Taking stress ticks segments.
- When filled: PC is **taken out for the scene**. Clock clears.

### Condition
- Descriptive text written below the stress clock.
- Updated as stress clock fills.
- Imposes **narrative restrictions** on what the PC can/cannot do.

### Recovery
- **Only in-fiction**: rest, lay low, or seek medical attention.
- GM judges fiction and clears **1–4 stress**.
- Update condition to match improved state.

*Designer guidance*: Clocks can represent other expendable resources (reputation, money, corruption). Design questions: How filled? What happens when full? How cleared?

---

## 10. PROGRESSION

### Session End
- Players update **details** to reflect growth.

### Milestones
- GM awards after **big story events**.
- Invest in **talent tree**.
- Talent = +1 action dot OR situational talent.

*Designer guidance*: Milestone frequency controls power curve. Short campaign = frequent milestones. Long campaign = scarce milestones + detail evolution as primary growth.

---

## 11. FORTUNE ROLLS

Used by GM to disclaim decision-making (PCs not directly involved) or by players when no other roll applies.

**Building the pool:**
- Use a specific action rating, OR
- Add **1d6 per likeliness level** of the thing happening.

**Resolution:**
- **66**: Critical success. Great results + something exceptional.
- **6**: Full success. Great results.
- **4–5**: Partial success. Mixed results.
- **1–3**: Bad outcome. Bad results.

**No momentum generated. No consequences.**

---

## 12. FLASHBACKS

Players can declare they prepared for a situation retroactively (cannot undo established narrative).

**Procedure:**
1. GM sets cost: **0–2 momentum**.
2. Player pays cost.
3. Player narrates the flashback scene.
4. GM decides resolution:
   - No roll needed (direct narrative impact).
   - Action roll (success uncertain).
   - Fortune roll (success was luck-based).

---

## 13. WORLD FORCES

Independent organizations with agendas that make the world vibrant.

**Structure per force:**
- **Goal**: What they want to achieve (not inherently good/bad).
- **Methods**: 2–3 ways they interact with the world to achieve it.
- **Progress clock**: GM creates one if it makes sense; ticks when narrative supports it (consequences or player actions helping the force).

*Example format*: **[Force Name]** want to **[Goal]** by: [Method 1], [Method 2], [Method 3].

--- 

## 14. GM PRINCIPLES

- **Be a fan of the players.** Facilitate interesting stories; don't trap them.
- **Don't plan, play.** No exhaustive prep; play to find out what happens.
- **When in doubt, ask questions.** Use player ideas to fill the world.
- **This is a game, not a simulation.** Prioritize fun and cool over hyper-realism.

---

## 15. EXTRAS

### Attachment Extra
- An **attachment** is a character **detail** that can be **broken** to **cancel a consequence outright** (no determination roll).
- Must make narrative sense.
- Broken attachments cannot be used until repaired/reacquired.
- Repair: Start a **4-segment (or more) project clock**. Can only roll progress during appropriate downtime.
- When project completes, attachment is restored and can be broken again.

## 18. ACTION-IF IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

This section documents how the Charge SRD maps to the Action-IF engine implementation.

### Health Model: Guard + Body

The engine uses **Guard + Body** instead of the Charge stress clock:
- **Guard** (default 3, max `maxGuard` up to 5) absorbs harm first and resets to `maxGuard` after every scene.
- **Body** (default 6, persistent) is the character's actual health. When Body drops to 0 or below, the character is incapacitated (`harmPenalty` returns −1 for rolls, actions disabled).
- `harmPenalty(character)` returns −1d / −2d / −1 based on Body level: ≥5 = 0, 3–4 = −1d, 1–2 = −2d, ≤0 = −1 (incapacitated).
- **Stress is not implemented.** Guard serves as the per-scene buffer; Body is the persistent resource.

### Effect System

- Effect levels: **Great** (3 ticks), **Standard** (2), **Limited** (1), **None** (0).
- Critical success doubles effect-level ticks.
- Partial success has a **50/50** outcome: either full effect + designer's consequence, or reduced effect (one level lower, no consequence).
- Reduced Effect is automatic system behavior — game designers do NOT choose it.
- Stunts and items can have `bonusEffect` to increase the effective effect level.
- Push for effect (`push effect <action>`) upgrades effect by one level instead of +1d.

### Position / Risk

**Not implemented.** The engine has no controlled/risky/desperate position system. All action entries provide explicit consequences that apply on failure (always) and partial success (50% of the time).

### Determination / Resistance Rolls

**TBD.** The current `resistanceRoll` uses the character's highest action rating. The full momentum-based determination roll system (spend momentum for dice, reduce/avoid consequences) is not yet implemented.

### Teamwork

Three maneuvers are implemented:
- **Assist**: 1 momentum → +1d to ally's roll (pre-roll step).
- **Protect**: Redirect a teammate's consequence to yourself (no reduction — determination is TBD).
- **Set Up**: Roll an action for indirect benefit. On success, the next teammate chooses +1d or +effect.

### Add Tension

**Not implemented.** Too subjective for code.

### Talents

Talents are implemented as **Stunts** — defined in the top-level `stunts` array, referenced by ID from characters and crew.
